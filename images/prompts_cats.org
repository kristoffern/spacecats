* How to use this
Anthropomorphic uniformed crew-cat headshots for the demo app. The STYLE block is
sent with every image; only the Subject changes per slot, which is what keeps the 25
portraits one consistent set. Generate with =python generate.py cats all=, curate on a
contact sheet, regenerate weak slots, then =python resize_accept.py cats=.

#+begin_style
Style: a polished sci-fi character portrait in a clean painterly digital-illustration
style, friendly and characterful. A single ANTHROPOMORPHIC cat crew member, shown
head-and-shoulders like an ID portrait, wearing a fitted starship uniform. Keep the art
style, lighting, uniform design and framing identical across the whole set; only the cat
and its expression change.

Uniform: a dark navy starship crew uniform with a gold shoulder yoke and a small plain
gold collar tab, in a Star Trek gold-and-blue palette. The same uniform on every cat. No
visible writing or numbers anywhere on it.

Background: a simple, softly out-of-focus starship-bridge interior in deep navy
(#0d1b2a), gentle rim light and a faint warm glow. Uncluttered, no readable panels.

Framing: a centred head-and-shoulders portrait, the cat facing forward or in a slight
three-quarter turn, looking confident and alert.

Exclusions: no text, no numerals, no name tags, no written badges, no borders or frame,
no photograph, no humans. Exactly one cat.
#+end_style
