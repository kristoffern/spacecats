* How to use this
Deep-space mission backdrops for the demo app. The STYLE block is sent with every image;
only the Subject changes per slot. Generate with =python generate.py missions all=,
curate, then =python resize_accept.py missions=.

#+begin_style
Style: a cinematic deep-space scene, rich painterly sci-fi illustration, atmospheric and
detailed. Keep the rendering style, palette and mood identical across the whole set; only
the subject and framing change.

Palette: deep navy and black space (#0d1b2a) with gold, amber and cyan highlights,
matching a gold-and-blue starship theme. Clean, not cluttered with lens flares.

Composition: 16:9 horizontal, the main subject off-centre with generous dark empty space
around it, so it reads as an atmospheric backdrop.

Exclusions: no text, no numerals, no labels, no people, no spaceships unless the subject
says so, no borders or frame, no UI overlays.
#+end_style
